## 2.1 Recognised merchant disputes
When the customer recognises the merchant but disputes fulfilment, such as goods or services not received, a subscription or order cancelled but not refunded, or an incorrect amount charged, the case is a merchant dispute rather than fraud. Recommend the Dispute Operations queue. Do not route recognised-merchant service complaints to fraud investigation unless independent fraud anomalies are also present.

## 2.2 Evidence for merchant disputes
For merchant disputes the analyst should record the order or cancellation date reported by the customer, the transaction amount and status, and whether the merchant was contacted. Missing cancellation confirmation or delivery evidence must be listed as missing information for the reviewer.

## 2.3 Incorrect amount
If the customer recognises the merchant but states the amount charged differs from the agreed amount, record both values as stated by the customer and the ledger amount from the transaction record. Route to Dispute Operations for merchant chargeback assessment.
