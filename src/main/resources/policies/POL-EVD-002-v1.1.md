## 1.1 Mandatory evidence
A payment dispute cannot be classified unless the transaction record is available and the customer statement has been captured. When the transaction record is not found, a source system is unavailable or times out, or the customer statement is missing, classify the case as insufficient evidence and refer it for manual review. Never substitute assumed values for unavailable evidence.

## 1.2 Customer-authorised activity indicators
When the customer disputes a transaction but the evidence shows a trusted long-standing device, a location consistent with recent activity, a successful authentication challenge and a history of similar disputes, the activity may be genuine customer-authorised use. Such cases are referred for manual review; a human reviewer confirms the outcome and no customer-facing decision is automated.

## 1.3 Absent policy guidance
If no approved policy passage applies to the case, the assistant must state that applicable guidance was not found, must not describe a procedure, and must refer the case for manual review.
